# Difficulty guide

Five levels, from the player's point of view. The examples come from the real question bank
(`data/questions/`). Difficulty is about **how obscure the fact is** and **how close the
distractors are**, never about confusing wording.

| Level         | Who gets it right                                    | The fact                                         | Distractors                                    |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| `muy fácil`   | Almost anyone, including children                    | Everyday general knowledge                       | Clearly distinct from the answer               |
| `fácil`       | Most adults without studying the topic               | Well known, taught at school                     | Plausible but distinguishable by common sense  |
| `medio`       | Someone with some interest or knowledge of the topic | A fact a keen amateur knows                      | Plausible; need real knowledge to rule out     |
| `difícil`     | A fan or a good student of the topic                 | A specific fact: a precise date, a title, a name | Very close: same era, same field, same style   |
| `muy difícil` | Experts, or a lucky guess                            | A very specific, little-known detail             | Extremely close: adjacent years, near-homonyms |

## muy fácil

General knowledge that most people have.

> ¿Cuál es el planeta más grande del sistema solar? Júpiter / Saturno / Neptuno / Urano

> ¿Cuántos jugadores tiene cada equipo sobre el campo en un partido de fútbol? 9 / 10 / 11 / 12

## fácil

> ¿Cuál es la capital de Australia? Canberra / Sídney / Melbourne / Brisbane

> ¿Cuál es el símbolo químico del oro? Au / Ag / Fe / Pt

## medio

Needs some knowledge of the subject.

> ¿En qué año cayó el Muro de Berlín? 1987 / 1989 / 1990 / 1991

> ¿Quién dirigió «Todo sobre mi madre» (1999)? Pedro Almodóvar / Alejandro Amenábar /
> Fernando Trueba / Carlos Saura

## difícil

Genuinely hard: a specific fact, and distractors from the same family.

> ¿Qué tratado puso fin a la Guerra de los Treinta Años en 1648? Paz de Westfalia /
> Paz de Augsburgo / Tratado de Utrecht / Paz de los Pirineos
>
> All four are famous European peace treaties; only someone who knows the period can pick.

> ¿Qué película de Luis Buñuel obtuvo la Palma de Oro de Cannes en 1961? Viridiana /
> El ángel exterminador / Belle de jour / Tristana
>
> All four are real Buñuel films; the year is what decides.

## muy difícil

Very specific facts, with distractors that are almost the answer.

> ¿En qué año se concedió por primera vez el Premio Cervantes? 1974 / 1975 / 1976 / 1977
>
> 1975 is the trap: that is when the prize was created. Close years are the right kind of
> distractor.

> ¿Cuál es el número atómico del wolframio? 72 / 73 / 74 / 75
>
> The distractors are the neighbouring elements.

> ¿Quién fue el primer ciclista que ganó cinco veces el Tour de Francia? Eddy Merckx /
> Jacques Anquetil / Bernard Hinault / Miguel Induráin
>
> The distractors also won five Tours, but later: the word "primer" is what matters, and each
> distractor was checked to be later.

## Making a hard question hard (and keeping it fair)

- Ask for a **precise** fact (a year, a title, a name, a number), not a vague association.
- Build distractors from the **same family** as the answer: neighbouring years, works by the
  same author, rivals from the same era, adjacent figures.
- Keep distractors **verifiably wrong**. A hard question with two defensible answers is a
  broken question, not a hard one.
- Do not make it hard with wording: no negations, no stacked clauses, no "excepto".
- A `difícil` or `muy difícil` question that a general reader would answer on the first guess
  is mis-labelled. Raise the specificity or lower the label.

## Bad distractors (do not write these)

| Problem                         | Example                                              | Why it fails                          |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Different type                  | ¿Quién pintó «Las Meninas»? Velázquez / 1656 / ...   | Mixes a person with a year            |
| Obviously absurd                | ... / Napoleón / un delfín / ...                     | Nobody is tempted; wastes a slot      |
| Gives the answer away by length | `Sí` / `No` / `Diego Rodríguez de Silva y Velázquez` | The long one is clearly the answer    |
| Also correct                    | Capital de Bolivia: La Paz / Sucre / ...             | Two defensible answers; ambiguous     |
| Time-sensitive                  | ¿Quién es el presidente de...?                       | Becomes wrong without anyone noticing |
